Why mobile

Grooming should feel safe, not stressful.

After years working in the grooming industry, Ryan built Dunk'N Dogs around a simple belief: grooming should be a calm, one-on-one experience. The salon comes to your door, so your dog never has to wait their turn.

i.

No cages, ever

No crates and no holding pens. Your dog stays with the groomer from start to finish.

ii.

Just one pup

Each appointment is focused on one dog at a time, so they get the patience and attention they deserve.

iii.

Right at home

No drop-offs, no long waits, no car rides. The fully equipped van parks in your driveway.

The groomer
Hi, I'm Ryan,

owner & groomer of Dunk'N Dogs.

I've been grooming dogs for nearly 20 years and have always believed grooming should be a calm, one-on-one experience for pets. After years in the industry, I had the chance to start my own mobile business so I could offer dogs a more comfortable, low-stress groom right at home.

Skilled with all kinds of breeds, I'm happy to work to your specifications and talk through styles with you, so you never get a surprising result.
